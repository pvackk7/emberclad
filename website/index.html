THE TALE

The flame that emerged within a mere suit of armor–resembling that of a knight’s–would be the only thing keeping it alive. The knight knew no purpose, but to keep its fire stoked by slaying its enemies. Cresting a hill that transcended the forest, it espied a gothic castle jutting from the horizon, a formidable dragon looming over it.

Though, between the knight and the foreboding castle was a long journey. While the knight’s purpose was unknown, aside from its means to sustain a cataclysmic flame, it grew curious as to what it was or where it came from. Conceiving that the castle had answers, it fated to wander toward it.
